## AI3 field origin map (what populates missing-fields report)
- AI3 maps LLM `unified_file` payload to `UnifiedFileBase` (payment_type, currency, exchange_rate, gross/net, credit card fields, etc.) in `backend/src/services/ai_service.py:946-1005` (`run_ai3_data_extraction`).
- `_persist_extraction_result` writes those fields into `unified_files` (including credit_card_last_4_digits and credit_card_brand_short) in `backend/src/api/ai_processing.py:527-555`.
- Receipt items are required in AI3 response (`backend/src/services/ai_service.py:1011-1040`) and are persisted via `_replace_receipt_items` (`backend/src/api/ai_processing.py:317-377`).

## AI4 parsing + current validation
- Per-entry validation (account_code, debit/credit non-zero) happens in `parse_accounting_proposals` and `_build_accounting_proposal` (`backend/src/services/ai_service.py:190-384`).
- Cross-entry validation enforces balanced debit/credit, account_code existence against chart_of_accounts, and totals reconciliation when gross/net/vat are present in `_validate_accounting_proposals` (`backend/src/services/ai_service.py:393-442`), invoked in `run_ai4_accounting_classification` (`backend/src/services/ai_service.py:1127-1227`).
